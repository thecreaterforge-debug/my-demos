Questions

Things people often ask

Is your gold hallmarked?

Yes. Gold jewellery carries the BIS hallmark, and we show it to you at the time of purchase.

How are making charges decided?

Making charges depend on the design. We tell you the percentage before you buy, and it is written separately on your bill.

Can I exchange my old gold?

Yes. We weigh and test your old gold in front of you and adjust its value against your new purchase.

Can I get the price without visiting?

Yes. Send the design or weight on WhatsApp and we will share today's approximate price. The final price is confirmed in the showroom.
